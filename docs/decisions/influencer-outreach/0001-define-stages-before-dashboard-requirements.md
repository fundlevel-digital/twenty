## Decision

Partial Information, Ghosted and Disqualified. Discuss their meanings and
requirements incrementally, beginning with Cold and Ghosted, and document the
resulting choices.

## Why

settling what needs to appear on the dashboards.

## Consequences

and exit rules, stage transitions, required data and dashboard contents remain
undecided. The list does not establish a sequential funnel or automatic transitions.
